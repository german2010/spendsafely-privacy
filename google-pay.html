Google Pay sync

SpendSafely Pro · Android · Contact: phstories0@gmail.com

Pay with Google Pay on this phone, and SpendSafely records the purchase for you. It reads the shop and the amount from Google Wallet's purchase notification. Everything stays on your phone.

Before you start

  • SpendSafely Pro is active.
  • You pay with this phone through Google Wallet.
  • Google Wallet's purchase notifications are on.

Turn it on

  1. Profile → turn on Google Pay.
  1. Tap Continue.
  1. Tap Allow next to Notification access (required).
  1. In Android's settings, turn on Allow notification access (this screen differs a little between phone makers).
  1. Tap Allow, then go back to SpendSafely.
  1. Tap Allow next to SpendSafely notifications, then Allow again. The app uses them to tell you about a new shop or a payment it can't record by itself. Battery: unrestricted is optional; it helps on Samsung and Xiaomi.
  1. Tap Turn on.

After a purchase

  • New shop: recorded under Others. Tap the notification to pick a category.
  • Known shop: recorded quietly with its last category (still Others if you never changed it). Shops starting with the same word share it, e.g. every “MCDONALDS …”.
  • Amount shown as 0, or another currency: nothing is guessed. The payment waits on Home until you enter what you were charged.

Why a purchase is missing

  • Google Wallet showed no notification for it (common at fuel pumps and some terminals). Check Wallet's history.
  • You paid with the card, not the phone.
  • Sync was off or Pro wasn't active at the time. Those purchases aren't added later; add them by hand.
  • Notification access was removed. Profile shows “No access to notifications — tap to allow”.
  • Google Wallet's notifications are off.

Privacy

Only Google Wallet purchase notifications are read, and only the shop and amount. Nothing is logged or sent anywhere. When sync is off or Pro isn't active, the app doesn't listen at all. Privacy Policy

Turn it off

Profile → turn off Google Pay. To also remove the access, go to Android Settings → Notifications → Device & app notifications → SpendSafely (names differ by phone maker).
