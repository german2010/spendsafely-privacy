Data Deletion

How to request deletion of your data in SpendSafely (com.polapps.spendsafely), developed by Herman Politov · Contact: phstories0@gmail.com

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to phstories0@gmail.com with the subject "SpendSafely data deletion request".
  2. If you made a Pro purchase, include its Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.). You'll find it in the receipt email from Google Play, or in the Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history. We use it to find the pseudonymous purchase record; without it we cannot tell which record is yours.
  3. Uninstall SpendSafely before you send the request. If the app stays installed, Google Play hands your purchase back to it on its next start, and a new purchase record is created.
  4. We will confirm the deletion by reply within 30 days (usually much sooner).

Transactions (including shops recorded by Google Pay sync), categories, notes, settings, display name Only on your device Never held by us. Delete it yourself at any time by uninstalling the app or using Android's "Clear data". Backups/exports you created are files under your control — delete them where you saved them.
